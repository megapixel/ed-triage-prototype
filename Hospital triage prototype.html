Hospital triage prototype

A clickable rebuild of the iOS triage and consultation app, using mock data. Pick a starting point.

Where this came from

  • Screens and flow from the iOS storyboards (Patient and Doctor)
  • Reference lists from the Laravel seeders: P1 to P4, coverage areas, bed classes, admission disciplines, doctor roles
  • Beacon room tracking is simulated, with no real hardware or beacons
  • Not included: face capture, real login
